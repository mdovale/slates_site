1About

Slates is a simple app.

It gives you eight color-coded scratchpads so you can capture text fast and find it by color: door codes, meeting links, snippets to paste a few times.

Stored locally by default; optional iCloud sync if you want it.

2The desk

Eight slates. That’s the whole map.

Keep a list in one color, a code in another, a draft in a third. You remember which slate, not which folder. Hide the ones you are not using; they keep their text.

3On your Mac

Capture, copy, clear.

Hold a door code, a tracking number, a paragraph. Copy it when you need it. Copy & Clear when it has done its job, and the slate is empty again.

Press ⌘3Command-3 to focus slate 3, and ⌘\Command-Backslash to see all of them again.

4Dark and Light

Same desk. Lights up.

Every slate keeps its color in Dark and in Light. Slates follows your Mac, or you pick under View → Theme. Drag across the screen to compare.

5In the world

Wherever the desk is.

Slates on real Macs, in real rooms. Swipe or scroll sideways.

One feature for every color.

  • 1Color first

    Each slate has its own color: header, card, chrome. Organize by hue, not folders.

  • 2Copy & Clear

    Copy in one click. Copy & Clear for one-time codes.

  • 3Plain, Rich, Code

    Write how you need: plain text, rich Markdown, or syntax-highlighted code.

  • 4Menu bar or Dock

    Live in the Dock, the menu bar, or both.

  • 5Optional iCloud

    Sync is off until you turn it on. Your slates, your Mac, unless you say otherwise.

  • 6Keyboard native

    Navigation, find, indent, multi-cursor: a scratchpad that types like a Mac editor.

  • 7Backups

    Automatic local snapshots, plus Save Backup and Restore when you want a file.

  • 8Private by design

    No analytics. No third-party sync. Just the notes you typed.
