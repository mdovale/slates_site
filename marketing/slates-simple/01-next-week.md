# **Notes**

**Slate | sleɪt |**
_noun_
**1.** _Geology._ A fine-grained rock easily split into smooth, flat pieces.

**2.** A writing tablet of this stone. The useful property is not permanence. It is that you can fill it, read it, and clear it. Hence **a clean slate**.

**3.** _Informal._ A colored scratchpad. You remember the color, the way you remember which mug is yours.
