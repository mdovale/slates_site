## Description

Limit: 4,000 characters. Lead with the job, then features, then privacy.

```
Slates is a quiet Mac scratchpad for the bits of text you do not want to lose to a browser tab, a meeting chat, or the bottom of Notes.

You get eight color-coded slates in one window. Keep a draft in one color, a door code in another, a list in a third. Drag a card by its header and the color travels with it, so your mental map stays put.

WHAT IT’S FOR

Door codes, tracking numbers, meeting links, packing lists, a paragraph you are still chewing on, a snippet you will paste three times and then clear.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Write in Rich, Plain, or Code — headings and emphasis that actually render, syntax highlighting, and smart bullets that click through a cycle
• Copy any slate with one click, or Copy & Clear when a one-time code has done its job
• Drag slates by their headers to reorder; hide the ones you are not using
• Focus one slate, or see the whole set as a vertical list or a horizontal strip
• Wrap long lines, fit a slate to its content, or collapse the window to its toolbar

PRIVACY

Stored locally on your Mac by default. Optional iCloud sync stays off until you turn it on in Settings. Slates does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.
```

## Privacy nutrition-label wording notes

This is wording for the App Store privacy labels and the listing, **not** the App Store Connect form itself. Confirm against the shipping build before submit.

- **Tracking:** none. No analytics, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking.
- **Analytics data:** not collected.
- **Contact Info / Identifiers / Usage Data / Diagnostics:** not collected by Slates. No telemetry.
- **User Content:** slate text is stored on device (Application Support) by default. If the user enables **Settings → General → Sync with iCloud**, the same JSON is stored in the app’s iCloud Documents container so slates can sync across the user’s Macs.
- Do not check “data used for tracking” or “data linked to identity” for advertising.
- Do not claim “everything stays on your Mac” without mentioning optional iCloud.
- Expired / cleared text is removed from the store file; there is no cloud analytics pipeline.
