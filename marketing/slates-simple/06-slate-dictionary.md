# slate
**/sleɪt/**
_noun_ · _verb_

**1.** _Geology._ A fine-grained rock that splits into thin plates. Roofing, flooring, and the small tablets children once wrote on and wiped away.

**2.** A writing tablet of this stone. The useful property is not permanence. It is that you can fill it, read it, and clear it. Hence **a clean slate**.

**3.** _Informal._ A colored scratchpad among a small, fixed set. You remember the color, the way you remember which mug is yours.

## verb

**1.** To designate or schedule. _The site is slated for Friday._

**2.** _Chiefly British._ To criticize severely.

---
Middle English from Old French _esclate_, a splinter. Related: **clean slate**, _tabula rasa_, [scratchpad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scratchpad).
