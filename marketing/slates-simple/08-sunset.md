# **Sunset**

Lagoon to coral. Warm afternoon light. Eight steps. Do not skip.

`#3e9cbf`  `#72c5d7`  `#a7ecf2`  `#d9e4b0`  `#f2c43d`  `#f1a33a`  `#f17c37`  `#f26d50`

Straw `#d9e4b0` is the hinge. Gold `#f2c43d` is noon, not body. Coral `#f26d50` is the last light. Lagoon `#3e9cbf` holds the sky.
