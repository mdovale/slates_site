## Eight slates

Slates is not a library. It is eight pads in one window.

Use one for a meeting, one for a list, one for a snippet you will paste three times. Hide the ones you are not using. Focus a single slate when you want the rest out of the way. Switch the list from a stack to a strip when the desk is wide.

The colors are the organization. There are no tags, notebooks, or nested folders to maintain.

Photography: the **desk pack** in `../slates/` is the eight pads for the site hero — one Bay Area engineer’s desk, including a Palatino dictionary entry for the word _slate_. ## Privacy

Slates stores your text locally by default. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, slates live in your iCloud Documents container so they can follow you to another Mac. Sync stays off until you opt in.

There is no analytics SDK, no third-party sync service, and no advertising.

## FAQ

### Why only eight?

Because eight is a desk, not an archive. If a pad has done its job, copy it out and clear it. If you need a library, you already have Notes.

### Is this a notes app?

It is a scratchpad. The text persists until you clear it, but the point is the eight pads you keep in view — not a filing system, search corpus, or timeline.

### How is this different from Apple Notes?

Notes is a library: folders, attachments, sync as a default, a place for things you mean to keep. Slates is eight color-coded pads for the scraps that do not deserve a note. Different job. They can sit next to each other.

### What about Tot?

Tot is a fine, small companion with seven colored dots. Slates is eight pads on a Mac, with Rich and Code modes, clickable cycles, and hex previews — still a scratchpad, not a document app. If Tot already fits your desk, you do not need both. If you want syntax highlighting, smart bullets, or a Mac-first window you can strip and snap, that is the difference.

### Where does my text live?

On your Mac, in the app’s Application Support folder, unless you enable iCloud sync. Then it lives in iCloud Documents as well. Either way, Slates does not send it to us. We do not have analytics to send it to.

### Can I use it on iPhone?

This listing is for the Mac app. iPhone is out of scope for this page.
